B

Benoît Vermeersch

22 · Belgian · student · founder of MuscleUp

I did my first push-up at 15, and I've been doing push-ups, pull-ups and everything that comes after them ever since.

I'm a 22-year-old student from Belgium, building MuscleUp alongside my studies.

What never got easier was knowing what to do next. Calisthenics has no shortage of moves to chase, but almost no map showing which one follows which — so you end up guessing, plateauing, or trying something your shoulders aren't ready for.

So I decided to build that map as a skill tree, for anyone who's been stuck in the same place. Every move is a node, every node is unlocked by the one before it, and getting stronger becomes a matter of following the line instead of hunting for it. Five branches, a hundred-odd moves, reps that count towards something, and a leaderboard so it's not just you against the wall. It's early, it's rough in places, and it gets better most weeks.
